One line, 1 to 240 characters, that gives the judge a quiet, earned moment
of pride for someone in a scene — a small person doing something hard, a
long effort finally landing. Built for the make.fun bounty
[One Line of Earned Pride](https://make.fun/c/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557).

`main.py` scores five candidate lines against the judge's five criteria
(genuine earned pride, concrete scene, pride earned inside the line,
lands in one read, freshness) and writes the winner to
`/output/entry.txt`.

Winning line:

> For six months she carried water up the hill before sunrise; today the tap in her village ran, and she stood there with her hands still cupped.
